PrivacyPrywatność

This is a private, non-commercial website. It does not use cookies, analytics or contact forms and does not collect any personal data.

Your browser stores only your language choice (EN/PL) and the last map view in its local storage. You can clear it at any time in the browser settings.

Some pages show content from other services: band conditions images (HamQSL), the weather map (Windy.com, loaded only when you scroll to it), place search (Open-Meteo) and map libraries (jsDelivr). Those services receive your IP address like any website you visit. Links to YouTube, QRZ.com and other sites open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.
